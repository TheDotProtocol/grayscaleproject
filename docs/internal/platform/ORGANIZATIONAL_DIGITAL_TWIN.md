## Sprint 3 Phase B Alignment

The Digital Twin is **not** a database mirror. It is assembled from Bedrock context:

- Memory, Graph, Strategy, Intent, Temporal, Signals, Insights
- Organizational Intelligence, Executive Council, Attention Engine
- Founder Constitution, Organizational Operating Model

`CompanyContext.organizationalTwin` and `CompanyContext.twin` reference the same assembled snapshot.

Executives reason from the Twin — not from storage.
