## I. Purpose

The Living Organizational Twin (LOT) is the organization's living operating model across time. It is not a dashboard model. It is not a database snapshot. It is the single source of organizational truth for replay, current state, simulation, scenario planning, forecast intelligence, and learning.

The organization—not software components—owns the twin. No executive owns the twin. Every future executive reasons through the Living Organizational Twin.

---

## II. Constitutional Principles

1. **Organizational representation** — The twin represents the organization, not software components.
2. **Time-awareness** — The twin exists across past, present, and future projections.
3. **Versioning** — Every twin state is versioned and immutable once recorded.
4. **Immutability** — Historical twin states are never modified; new versions supersede.
5. **Reconstructability** — Every historical organizational state is reconstructable from events.
6. **Simulation isolation** — Simulations never modify reality.
7. **Forecast as hypothesis** — Forecasts are hypotheses, not facts.
8. **Reality supremacy** — Reality always supersedes simulation and forecast.
9. **Learning loop** — Learning compares predicted outcomes against actual outcomes.
10. **Executive reasoning** — Executives reason through the twin; they do not own it.
11. **Identity persistence** — Organizational identity persists across time.
12. **Constitutional evolution** — Twin evolution is constitutional and auditable.
