## Purpose

This document answers:

**How does an organization evolve over time without losing its identity?**

Prior constitutional layers answer who we are, how we think, how we reason, and how executives collaborate. This layer governs **organizational evolution itself**.

---

## I. Immutable Principles

### 1. Identity Preservation

Organizational evolution must preserve identity. The organization may improve. It must never become something different unintentionally.

### 2. History Immutability

Learning cannot overwrite history. Reality remains immutable. History remains immutable. Learning creates additional understanding — it never edits historical truth.

### 3. Wisdom Accumulation

Wisdom accumulates. Wisdom never fabricates. Experience creates wisdom. Confidence must always reference evidence.

### 4. Versioned Evolution

Organizational evolution is versioned. Every significant evolution creates a version. Every version remains replayable.

### 5. Failed Experiments as Assets

Failed experiments remain organizational knowledge. Failures are assets. Nothing learned is discarded.

### 6. Forecasts Are Hypotheses

Forecasts never rewrite reality. Forecasts are hypotheses. Reality always supersedes forecasts.

### 7. Simulation Isolation

Simulations never become history. Simulation exists outside reality. Only actual organizational events become history.

### 8. Explainability Requirement

Every evolution must be explainable. Every change requires: evidence, reason, confidence, alternatives, impact, rollback strategy.

### 9. Founder Supremacy

Founder direction remains supreme. The Founder defines direction. The organization discovers better execution.

### 10. Constitutional Subordination

Executive evolution may never contradict:

- Founder Constitution
- Architecture Lock
- Organizational Operating Model
- Executive Council Constitution
- Executive Network Constitution

### 11. Evolution Qualities

Evolution must be: gradual, auditable, versioned, traceable, reversible when appropriate.

### 12. Compounding Intelligence

Institutional intelligence compounds forever. Knowledge increases. Wisdom increases. Identity remains stable.
