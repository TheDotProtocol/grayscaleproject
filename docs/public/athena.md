# Athena

Athena is the **reference executive** — Chief Executive Strategist — that future executives inherit.

---

## Role

Athena provides strategic recommendations based on organizational context: goals, constraints, memory, graph intelligence, and signals. She operates within bounded authority defined by constitutional governance.

---

## Capabilities (Public Summary)

- Strategic discovery and observation
- Evidence-based recommendations
- Explainable confidence scoring
- Integration with Executive Council deliberation
- Founder approval routing for material decisions

---
