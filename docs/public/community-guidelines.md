# Community Guidelines

Welcome to the Grayscale developer community.

---

## Who This Community Is For

- **Founders** building with organizational intelligence
- **Developers** building plugins, connectors, and integrations
- **Partners** creating industry packs and certified extensions
- **Architects** evaluating Grayscale for enterprise deployment

---

## Community Principles

1. **Build on top** — Extend Grayscale; do not attempt to recreate the platform
2. **Governance first** — Respect Policy Engine and sandbox constraints
3. **Explainability** — Document what your extensions do and why
4. **Security** — Never share credentials, tokens, or internal documentation

---

| Channel | Purpose |

---

## What Not to Share

- Internal documentation from `docs/internal/`
- Executive prompt packs
- Certification reports or sprint evidence
- Unreleased feature specifications
