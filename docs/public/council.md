# Executive Council

Structured deliberation for organizational decisions at scale.

---

## Overview

The Executive Council provides a **deterministic deliberation pipeline** where executives debate recommendations, review evidence, and produce auditable conclusions.

---

## Key Features

- Multi-stage deliberation workflow
- Searchable minutes and votes
- Council scheduling modes
- Immutable council memory
- Founder oversight on material outcomes

---

## When to Use

- Strategic priorities requiring multi-perspective analysis
- Decisions with organizational-wide impact
- Situations requiring documented deliberation for boards or compliance

---

## Access

Founder workspace: `/dashboard/council`
