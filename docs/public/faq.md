# FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Grayscale OS v1.0.

---

## What is Grayscale OS?

Grayscale OS is an **Organizational Operating System** — infrastructure for collective judgment, institutional memory, executive explainability, and constitutional governance. It is not a chatbot or generic productivity tool.

---

## What does "Bedrock" mean?

**Bedrock** is the codename for Grayscale OS v1.0 Foundation — the certified, frozen platform layer (event store, memory, graph, intelligence, Mission Control). Future capabilities extend Bedrock; they do not replace it.

---

## Is Grayscale open source?

Grayscale uses an **Open Platform** strategy: documented APIs and SDK for extensions, with commercial protection for the core OOS, cloud, and enterprise offerings. See the architecture overview for philosophy; full licensing is documented in platform strategy materials.

---

## Can I self-host Grayscale?

Local development and evaluation via Docker is supported today. Production self-hosted and on-premise deployments are **Enterprise** offerings — contact [sales@projectgrayscale.com](/contact).

---

## Are AI executives enabled by default?

**No.** `EXECUTIVES_ENABLED=false` by default. Executives require certification before activation. Autonomous execution is separately gated (`AUTONOMOUS_EXECUTION_ENABLED=false`).

---

## What is Mission Control?

Mission Control is the founder command surface — platform health, company readiness, widgets, timeline, and search in one operational view. [Learn more →](/docs/mission-control)

---

## What is CompanyContext?

A unified, read-only assembly of organizational state consumed by all capabilities. Prevents duplicate storage and ensures every recommendation traces to evidence.

---

## How does governance work?

The **Policy Engine** evaluates whether actions are permitted. The **Governance Kernel** is the final checkpoint before execution. Default deny. Explicit allow. Founder approval routing where required.

---

## Can developers build plugins?

Yes — via the [Plugin SDK](/docs/sdk) and connector model. Plugins integrate through approved ports; they do not bypass governance.
