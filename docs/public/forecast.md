# Foresight & Forecast

Forward-looking organizational intelligence with explainable outputs.

---

## Overview

Grayscale Foresight analyzes trends, signals, and organizational dynamics to surface forward-looking insights — always with explainability, never fabricated certainty.

---

## Key Features

- Trend analysis from organizational signals
- Forecast scenarios linked to simulation
- Confidence bounds (not point-estimate hallucination)
- Integration with Mission Control widgets

---

## Distinction

**Forecast** surfaces what the organization should **watch**.  
**Simulation** explores what happens if you **act**.
