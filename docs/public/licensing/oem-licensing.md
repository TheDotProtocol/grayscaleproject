# OEM Licensing

**Status:** Documentation placeholder

---

## Overview

OEM Licensing enables **platform partners** to embed Grayscale organizational capabilities within their own products under contractual terms.

---

## Use Cases

- Industry software vendors adding organizational intelligence
- Holding company platforms with portfolio-wide governance
- White-label organizational operating capabilities

---

## Not Included in Standard License

OEM requires separate commercial agreement covering:

- Branding and white-label terms
- Data residency requirements
- Liability and indemnification
- Support tier definitions
- Revenue share (if applicable)
