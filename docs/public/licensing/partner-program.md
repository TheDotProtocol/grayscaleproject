## Overview

The Partner Program enables certified organizations to build **executives, industry packs, connectors, and enterprise integrations** on Grayscale.

---

## Partner Types

| Type | Focus |
|------|-------|
| Technology Partner | Connectors and plugins |
| Executive Partner | Certified executive modules |
| Industry Partner | Vertical industry packs |
| Systems Integrator | Enterprise deployment and customization |

---

## Requirements

- Security and sandbox certification
- Policy Engine compliance
- Documentation and support commitment
- Commercial agreement
