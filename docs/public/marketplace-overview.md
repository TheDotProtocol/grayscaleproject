# Marketplace Overview

The Grayscale Marketplace (roadmap) enables certified third-party plugins and industry packs.

---

## Vision

A curated ecosystem where developers publish:

- **Connectors** — CRM, ERP, dev tools, industry data
- **Industry Packs** — Pre-configured goals, policies, and templates
- **Widget Bundles** — Mission Control dashboard extensions
- **Automation Templates** — Governance-approved policy packs

---

## Certification

All marketplace modules require:

1. Security review (sandbox escape, credential handling)
2. Policy compliance (default deny respected)
3. Integration health benchmarks
4. Documentation and support contact

---

## Revenue Model (Planned)

| Model | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Free | Open connectors and community templates |
| Paid | Premium industry packs and advanced automation |
| Revenue share | Partner program for certified publishers |
